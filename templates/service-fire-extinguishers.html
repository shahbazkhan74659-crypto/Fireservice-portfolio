What's Included

  • Facility hazard mapping and extinguisher placement plan
  • Supply of extinguishers for every hazard class
  • Mounting, signage and staff familiarization
  • Scheduled refilling and hydrostatic testing
  • AMC for ongoing upkeep

We provide suitable fire extinguishers for different types of fire hazards and applications — portable extinguishers are the first line of defense against a small fire before it spreads, covering Class A (ordinary combustibles), Class B (flammable liquids), Class C (gases), Class D (metals) and electrical hazards, each requiring the correct extinguishing agent.

Getting the extinguisher type and placement right for each hazard class isn't just a life-safety essential, it's also checked as part of Fire NOC compliance in Silvassa and Dadra & Nagar Haveli.

We map out the hazards across your facility, supply the correctly rated extinguishers, handle mounting and signage, and keep them compliant on a schedule of refilling and hydrostatic testing.

Using the wrong agent on the wrong hazard class doesn't just fail to put the fire out — a water-based extinguisher on an electrical fire, for example, can make the situation actively more dangerous. Correct classification at every point in your facility isn't optional.

Extinguishers also have a shelf life on their charge and a mandatory hydrostatic test interval on the cylinder itself, both of which are easy to lose track of across a large facility. We track both per unit so nothing on your premises quietly falls out of compliance.
