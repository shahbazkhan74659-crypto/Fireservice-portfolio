Safety Equipment

We supply and support a wide range of fire and personal safety equipment to help organizations maintain a safe working environment.

What's Included

  • Facility risk assessment for equipment needs
  • Supply of PPE, SCBA sets, hose reels and signage
  • Correct placement and mounting
  • Staff familiarization
  • AMC and replacement scheduling

We supply and support a wide range of fire and personal safety equipment — personal protective equipment (PPE), self-contained breathing apparatus (SCBA) sets, fire safety signage and hose reels — the equipment staff and emergency responders rely on during an actual incident, not just paperwork sitting in a cupboard.

The right equipment, correctly placed and clearly signed, helps maintain a safe working environment day to day and reduces response time when it's actually needed, and it's commonly checked during Fire NOC inspections.

We assess your facility's risk profile to determine what's needed, supply and position the equipment correctly, and brief your staff on how to use it.

SCBA sets in particular need their air cylinders checked and refilled on a schedule, not just stored and forgotten — equipment that's been sitting unchecked for months is exactly the kind that fails when someone actually reaches for it.

Signage and equipment placement also need to hold up under an actual evacuation, not just a walkthrough inspection — clear sightlines to hose reels and exit signage that's visible through smoke matter more in practice than they do on a floor plan.
