Our Process

Our Project Execution Process

Every project moves through the same seven phases — from first inquiry to handover and ongoing maintenance — so nothing gets missed along the way.

{% include "includes/consultationstats.html" %}
01

Phase 1

Initial Inquiry & Technical Risk Assessment

Customer Inquiry to On-Site Survey

Lead Qualification

Incoming call or tender enquiry captured via CRM. Customer requirements (building/industries type, hazard classification, timeline, and occupancy status) are logged.

Site Survey & Assessment

Field engineers conduct a site visit to assess physical dimensions, structural layouts, existing water pressure/flow rates, electrical infrastructure, and fire load (combustibles on site).

Hazard Analysis

Occupancy hazard level is classified under applicable codes (e.g., NFPA 13/14/72, NBC, local Civil Defence standards).

02

Phase 2

Design Engineering & Commercial Proposal

Calculations, Modeling & Budgeting

Hydraulic & Electrical Calculations

Engineers perform pipe sizing calculations (using Hazen-Williams formula) for suppression systems and battery/voltage-drop calculations for detection circuits.

Concept Design (CAD/BIM)

2D layouts and 3D BIM models are created, routing sprinkler mains, heat/smoke detectors, clean agent discharge nozzles, alarm panels and PA system while coordinating with MEP and structural teams to prevent clashes.

Estimation & Techno-Commercial Proposal

Bill of Quantities (BOQ) is developed covering piping, valves, fire pumps, detectors, panels, cabling, and labor cost estimates. The commercial proposal is submitted to the client for contract sign-off.

03

Phase 3

Code Approvals & Regulatory Permitting

AHJ & Civil Defence Submission

Shop Drawings Preparation

Standardized shop drawings, riser diagrams, cause-and-effect matrix, and equipment datasheets are compiled.

Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) Submission

Engineering documentation is formally submitted to local authorities (e.g., Civil Defence, municipal fire department) for regulatory review and permit issuance.

Design Revisions

Any feedback from AHJ code reviewers is incorporated into the drawing set before final permit approval.

04

Phase 4

Procurement, Quality Assurance & Off-Site Prep

Material Sourcing & Fabrication

Procurement

Orders are issued for UL-listed / FM-approved components (fire pumps, control panels, sprinklers, clean-agent cylinders, smoke detectors).

Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

Critical machinery like main fire pumps (electric and diesel drivers) undergo factory testing prior to site delivery.

Pre-fabrication

Header pipes, grooved pipe spools, and structural supports are cut and grooved off-site to accelerate installation timeline.

05

Phase 5

Site Installation & MEP Integration

Execution & Physical Civil/Electrical Work

Containment & Mechanical Installation

Installation of underground/aboveground fire mains, risers, zone valves, sprinkler drops, standpipes, and fire hose cabinets.

Electrical & Low-Voltage Wiring

Fire alarm control panels (FACP), addressable smoke/heat detectors, manual call points (MCP), strobes, and voice evacuation systems are installed.

MEP Interlocking

Integration with building services (HVAC trip-signals, stairwell pressurization fans, elevator recall systems, emergency door release, and access control override).

06

Phase 6

Testing, Commissioning (T&C) & Inspection

Hydrostatic, Cause-and-Effect & Final AHJ Sign-Off

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing

Piping networks undergo pressure testing (typically 200 psi for 2 hours per NFPA standards) to check for structural integrity and leaks.

Integrated System Testing (IST)

Simulation of real fire scenarios to verify that detection triggers alarms, shuts down HVAC dampers, releases clean agents, starts fire pumps, and sends remote signals to central monitoring.

AHJ Final Inspection & Certification

Civil Defence / Fire Department officials inspect the physical site, witness live tests, and issue the Fire Safety Completion Certificate / Occupancy Permit.

07

Phase 7

Handover, Training & Ongoing AMC

As-Built Documentation & Preventive Maintenance

Client Handover Package

Transfer of As-Built drawings, Operation & Maintenance (O&M) manuals, product warranty certificates, and test reports.

Facility Staff Training

Safety personnel and facility management are trained on operating the fire alarm panel, understanding system resets, emergency manual releases, and primary troubleshooting.

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

Transition into periodic inspection routines (quarterly, bi-annual, and annual checks) as required by life-safety regulations.
